## 绘制提示词

每张图沿用上述共同风格，具体提示词如下。

- **蕾拉·普莉兹姆利巴**　Layla Prismriver, human fourth daughter from Perfect Cherry Blossom's background text. No official outfit; an imaginative fan visualization. Ash-brown hair styled as a blunt rounded short bob with two neat side locks, dusty sage Victorian dress with a warm cream collar and a faded lavender waist ribbon. Looking thoughtfully at a small closed family album held to chest. Still human, no ghost effects, no instrument. Small calm smile and slight head tilt.
- **魂魄妖忌**　Youki Konpaku, the retired former Hakugyokurou gardener and swordsman from the Perfect Cherry Blossom profile. Appearance is a fan interpretation. Short swept-back silver hair tied in a very small low knot, short pointed gray beard, dignified gentle elderly face, muted moss green haori over an ivory kimono, blue-grey hakama and white tabi with sandals. One closed sheathed sword at waist. Hands visible and natural, a calm upright three-quarter stance. Include a small sprig of fallen cherry blossoms near his feet.
- **圣命莲**　Myouren Hijiri, human Buddhist monk and Byakuren Hijiri's younger brother mentioned in the Touhou Undefined Fantastic Object character profile. Imaginative fan portrayal. Shaved head clearly visible, warm round face and soft eyebrows, muted saffron robe and charcoal-brown kesa, small wooden prayer beads gathered loosely in the hands at waist, white tabi and dark sandals. Gentle serene smile, slightly bowed head, calm frontal standing pose. No purple hair, no fantasy effects.
- **大蛤蟆**　The giant toad, an actual amphibian monster mentioned in Bohemian Archive in Japanese Red. A friendly but stern huge Japanese toad, olive-grey pebbled skin with dark ochre flecks and a pale cream throat, oversized round eyes with horizontal pupils and round chunky head, its body in a grounded natural squat. Two forefeet naturally planted, rear feet tucked, complete silhouette. A couple reeds and tiny water rings for a pond suggestion, background mostly blank paper. No humanoid transformation or outfit, a charming creature sketch in the same cute proportions as the style reference.
